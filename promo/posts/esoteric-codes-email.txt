Subject: SlowLang - an esolang that enforces a 30 WPM typing cap

Hi Daniel - I built SlowLang for TinkerHub's Useless Projects 2.0 hackathon
(it won first place). The language is functionally Python, but the interface
is the joke: it enforces a ~30 WPM typing speed. Go faster and an angry ASCII
turtle locks you out for two seconds while error messages insult you in
haiku form. There's also a mandatory please() builtin checked at random
intervals, and a lazy mode that randomly refuses ~10% of valid programs.

The interesting bit is enforcement-as-aesthetic: the language spec exists
mainly to make the programmer behave badly on purpose. Happy to write it up
properly if it's a fit for the site.
